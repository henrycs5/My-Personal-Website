My Hobbies

Arting

As I mentioned before I'm an artist in the mediums of digital art and classic pencil drawings. A few months ago (Jul-Aug) I participated in an annual event called Art Fight (2026) which was also my first time doing it. On day one I created a schedule to draw other people's characters with the help of bookmarks. Initially I drew a minimum of two characters daily. When I realized that I was running out of time I switched to Mass Attacks which involved more than two characters. This decision helped me save a lot of time and stress from maintaining my art quality. In the end I finished two days before the event ended. For 2027 Art Fight I DO NOT want to draw 80+ characters especially while I'm in college. Perhaps I'll do a few that are a little more complex and out of my comfort zone!
